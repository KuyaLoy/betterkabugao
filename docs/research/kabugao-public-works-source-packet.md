# Kabugao Public Works Watch — source packet

Research review: **16 September 2026**
Scope: seven non-flood-control records suitable for a future, source-linked
Kabugao Public Works Watch. This is a research packet, not a claim that any
project is currently under construction or complete.

## Publication rule

Every value below comes from the linked primary DPWH or DBM record. Amounts
retain their source label. An **FY appropriation** is not an ABC, and neither is
an awarded contract amount. A CPES status is historical: it is only valid *as
reported on the evaluation date*.

The reviewed PhilGEPS notices for the Tuyangan road, water supply, evacuation
center, hospital building, Balay Pagdadarnan, and retaining wall are not in
this packet. Their notice pages do not establish an individual contractor or
contract amount, and PhilGEPS terms restrict copying and republishing site
content. Keep those notices as staff leads until a compliant award/contract
record or a direct permission basis is available.

## Fields deliberately unavailable

No row in this seven-record packet has a verified WGS84 project coordinate.
Station limits, route kilometer posts, and engineering grid coordinates are not
substitutes for a documented latitude/longitude. A first release must use
published locality text only; do not put these records on an interactive map.

The FY2026 GAA rows are final funding appropriations, not proof of procurement,
award, works start, delivery, or completion. The DPWH CPES rows provide a
contractor and historical status but do not prove the project remains in that
state today.

## Data-quality controls for a future implementation

- Keep the literal official reference when one is published; never create a
  replacement project ID.
- Show an amount-type badge: `FY appropriation` or `contract amount`.
- Display status only as “as reported on [CPES evaluation date].”
- Do not merge records merely because road names, station ranges, or contractors
  look similar. Use reference + year + station range as the review key.
- Retain a direct primary-source link and a review date for every visible row.
- PhilGEPS entries need a separate rights and award-verification review before
  inclusion.
