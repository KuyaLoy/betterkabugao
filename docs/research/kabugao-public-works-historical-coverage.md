# Kabugao Public Works Watch — historical coverage audit

Research review: **16 September 2026**

## Decision

The current 13-row register is a **curated, source-qualified starter**, not an
exhaustive count of Kabugao public works. It contains six DPWH flood-control
contracts that have official coordinates and seven non-flood records in the
[source packet](./kabugao-public-works-source-packet.md). Its 2022-onward shape
comes from the first available, consistently structured source set—not from
evidence that Kabugao had no earlier projects.

The public record can be defensibly extended back to **FY2015 appropriations**
and, for a corridor-level completion reference, **DPWH's 2010 annual report**.
This research found no primary, Kabugao-specific project record before 2000.
That is a discovery limit, not evidence that no earlier project existed.

## Staged expansion pipeline

1. **Protect the current scope.** Keep the 13 source-qualified records; do not
   call them comprehensive.
2. **Add the eight FY2015/FY2017 candidates as a separate “historic
   appropriation” layer** only after a manual review confirms the original PDF
   row and source URL. Never convert them into completed works.
3. **Search CPES reports and DPWH transparency/ArcGIS by exact route, station
   range, and reference** to find an award or completion counterpart. Add a
   candidate to the public register only when the reference or a documented
   matching key links the records.
4. **Use PhilGEPS only as a staff lead.** Request/locate the award, notice of
   award, contract, or implementing-agency disclosure before publication; do
   not copy notice text or treat the ABC as awarded value.
5. **Escalate older than 2015 through DPWH CAR / Apayao 1st DEO records and
   DILG FDP / COA document requests.** DILG's policy covers budgets, bids,
   contracts, and projects, but the portal did not yield retrievable Kabugao
   files in this pass. There is no evidence in this pass of a public,
   searchable municipal archive reaching the 1900s.

## Publication constraints

- Use `FY appropriation`, `ABC`, and `contract amount` as separate fields.
- Use official WGS84 geometry only; do not derive project pins from a barangay
  centroid, stationing, a road name, or an engineering grid without a stated
  coordinate reference system.
- Link primary documents and retain source/review dates. Do not mirror
  PhilGEPS notice text, documents, logos, or screenshots under the reviewed
  terms.
- A historic register should say “earliest online primary evidence found” — not
  “all projects since 2015” or “projects since the 1900s.”
